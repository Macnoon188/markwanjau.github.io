Bot Creation Phase 1 — Part 4

Where Jaspa's Story Gets Documented

Published: Monday, 18 May 2026  ·  Author: Mark Andrew Wanjau  ·  5 min read  ·  Tags: AI · Jaspa OS · Hermes · Engineering Journal

By the end of Part 3, something had become obvious: Jaspa had outgrown the blog format.

Weeks 14 through 17 documented the surface of the story — getting a bot running, teaching it to think like a technician, and eventually rebuilding the whole approach into something closer to real software engineering. But underneath those four posts was a much longer, much more detailed thread: the actual architecture decisions, the failed assumptions, the principles that got adopted along the way.

That thread deserved its own space. Not a blog post. A journal.

Why a Blog Post Wasn't Enough

A blog post works well for a single lesson — "here's what broke, here's what I learned." But Jaspa OS isn't a single lesson. It's dozens of them, stacked on top of each other, each one shaping the next.

Trying to compress that into blog-length posts meant constantly summarizing, skipping detail, and losing the actual engineering reasoning behind each decision. The parts that mattered most — the "why," not just the "what" — kept getting cut for length.

So instead of writing Part 5, Part 6, Part 7 as more blog posts, the decision was to build something that could hold the whole story properly: a page-by-page engineering journal, documenting Jaspa OS the way a real engineering team would document a platform.

What Changes, What Doesn't

This series — the Bot Development Series you've been reading since Week 14 — stays exactly where it is, in the AI & Automation section of the blog. Parts 1 through 4 tell the introductory arc: getting something running, giving it purpose, and realizing that purpose needed real engineering discipline behind it.

The deeper story — the architecture, the providers, the governance decisions, the acceptance tests — now lives in its own home under Projects, as a proper long-form engineering journal rather than a blog series.

The Engineering Journal

📓 Now Under Projects · Ongoing Series

Jaspa OS — Engineering Journal, Volume I

A page-by-page engineering notebook tracking the build of Jaspa OS from first principles through architecture, providers, and platform governance. Every entry follows a real engineering log format — Objective, Problem, Hypothesis, Implementation, Result, Lessons Learned — and closes with a live "State of Jaspa OS" progress readout. 72 planned pages, 24 published and counting.

Open the Notebook →

If you've been following along since Week 14 and want the full engineering depth behind Jaspa OS — not just the highlights — that's where the story continues.

Final Thoughts

Splitting the story this way felt right rather than tidy. The blog series is still the best place to read about the moments — the first Telegram response, the corrupted React component, the shift from prompting to engineering. The journal is where the actual system gets built, page by page, in the detail it deserves.

Part 4 closes this introductory arc. What comes next lives in the notebook.
